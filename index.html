Green Horizon — Layer Growth Model

From your starting flock to the target, reinvesting profit into new pullets. Change any number below and everything recalculates.

Assumptions

Cash never goes below zero: if your money can't cover the starting flock plus its running costs, the model buys fewer starting birds, and later purchases only happen when cash covers them (after the buffer).

Interest is entered as a yearly percentage. Laying curve = share of hens laying in each month since reaching lay age (Bovans Brown guide values from your workbook).

Month by month

The table runs from month 0 to the month the target flock is reached (highlighted). If the target is never reached, it shows all 200 months. Houses, labour and other bracket costs use last month's flock size, as in the workbook.

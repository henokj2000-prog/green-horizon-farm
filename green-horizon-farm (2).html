Meet the Owner
Henok Fikru
Founder & Farm Manager · Legetafo Legedadi, Ethiopia

Green Horizon Farm was born from a passion for sustainable agriculture and a belief that Ethiopia's land can feed its people abundantly. With a vision to create honest, healthy food while building a thriving business, I invested 1 million Birr into three complementary ventures — layer chickens, goat fattening, and beekeeping — all on my own land.

My goal is not just profit, but to create jobs, support local communities, and prove that modern small-scale farming in Ethiopia can be both profitable and sustainable.
